How It Works

  • Conference Overall (leaguewide) or a conference — Manhattan, Brooklyn.
  • Section A competitive section: K–1, Primary (K–3), or Elementary (K–5) — eligibility ceilings, not exclusive grade buckets.
  • School An ICN partner school competing as a team.
  • League Points Awarded by Matchday finish in that section (1st = 10, 2nd = 8, 3rd = 6, down to 8th+ = 1) and added up across the season — not the raw chess score from a single Matchday.
  • Trend Change since the last Matchday's standings.
